Surah Al-Baqara (Ayah 282) — Arabic, Translation & Spiritual Tafsir

Published 2026-11-13 Surah #2 4 min read
يَٰٓأَيُّهَا ٱلَّذِينَ ءَامَنُوٓا۟ إِذَا تَدَايَنتُم بِدَيْنٍ إِلَىٰٓ أَجَلٍۢ مُّسَمًّۭى فَٱكْتُبُوهُ ۚ وَلْيَكْتُب بَّيْنَكُمْ كَاتِبٌۢ بِٱلْعَدْلِ ۚ وَلَا يَأْبَ كَاتِبٌ أَن يَكْتُبَ كَمَا عَلَّمَهُ ٱللَّهُ ۚ فَلْيَكْتُبْ وَلْيُمْلِلِ ٱلَّذِى عَلَيْهِ ٱلْحَقُّ وَلْيَتَّقِ ٱللَّهَ رَبَّهُۥ وَلَا يَبْخَسْ مِنْهُ شَيْـًۭٔا ۚ فَإِن كَانَ ٱلَّذِى عَلَيْهِ ٱلْحَقُّ سَفِيهًا أَوْ ضَعِيفًا أَوْ لَا يَسْتَطِيعُ أَن يُمِلَّ هُوَ فَلْيُمْلِلْ وَلِيُّهُۥ بِٱلْعَدْلِ ۚ وَٱسْتَشْهِدُوا۟ شَهِيدَيْنِ مِن رِّجَالِكُمْ ۖ فَإِن لَّمْ يَكُونَا رَجُلَيْنِ فَرَجُلٌۭ وَٱمْرَأَتَانِ مِمَّن تَرْضَوْنَ مِنَ ٱلشُّهَدَآءِ أَن تَضِلَّ إِحْدَىٰهُمَا فَتُذَكِّرَ إِحْدَىٰهُمَا ٱلْأُخْرَىٰ ۚ وَلَا يَأْبَ ٱلشُّهَدَآءُ إِذَا مَا دُعُوا۟ ۚ وَلَا تَسْـَٔمُوٓا۟ أَن تَكْتُبُوهُ صَغِيرًا أَوْ كَبِيرًا إِلَىٰٓ أَجَلِهِۦ ۚ ذَٰلِكُمْ أَقْسَطُ عِندَ ٱللَّهِ وَأَقْوَمُ لِلشَّهَٰدَةِ وَأَدْنَىٰٓ أَلَّا تَرْتَابُوٓا۟ ۖ إِلَّآ أَن تَكُونَ تِجَٰرَةً حَاضِرَةًۭ تُدِيرُونَهَا بَيْنَكُمْ فَلَيْسَ عَلَيْكُمْ جُنَاحٌ أَلَّا تَكْتُبُوهَا ۗ وَأَشْهِدُوٓا۟ إِذَا تَبَايَعْتُمْ ۚ وَلَا يُضَآرَّ كَاتِبٌۭ وَلَا شَهِيدٌۭ ۚ وَإِن تَفْعَلُوا۟ فَإِنَّهُۥ فُسُوقٌۢ بِكُمْ ۗ وَٱتَّقُوا۟ ٱللَّهَ ۖ وَيُعَلِّمُكُمُ ٱللَّهُ ۗ وَٱللَّهُ بِكُلِّ شَىْءٍ عَلِيمٌۭ ﴿282﴾

English Translation (Sahih International)
"O you who have believed, when you contract a debt for a specified term, write it down. And let a scribe write [it] between you in justice. Let no scribe refuse to write as Allah has taught him. So let him write and let the one who has the obligation dictate. And let him fear Allah, his Lord, and not leave anything out of it. But if the one who has the obligation is of limited understanding or weak or unable to dictate himself, then let his guardian dictate in justice. And bring to witness two witnesses from among your men. And if there are not two men [available], then a man and two women from those whom you accept as witnesses - so that if one of the women errs, then the other can remind her. And let not the witnesses refuse when they are called upon. And do not be [too] weary to write it, whether it is small or large, for its [specified] term. That is more just in the sight of Allah and stronger as evidence and more likely to prevent doubt between you, except when it is an immediate transaction which you conduct among yourselves. For [then] there is no blame upon you if you do not write it. And take witnesses when you conclude a contract. Let no scribe be harmed or any witness. For if you do so, indeed, it is [grave] disobedience in you. And fear Allah. And Allah teaches you. And Allah is Knowing of all things."

Spiritual Context & Meaning

Surah Al-Baqara holds profound significance in Islamic spirituality, offering timeless reassurance to believers. In these sacred verses (Ayah 282), the Divine address directly touches the human heart, speaking directly to our inner struggles, trials, and search for spiritual grounding. When life presents overwhelming hardships, turning to the revelation provides clarity, reminding the believer of Allah's infinite wisdom, boundless mercy, and watchful care over all affairs.

Practical Dua for Peace & Barakah:
رَبَّنَا آتِنَا فِي الدُّنْيَا حَسَنَةً وَفِي الآخِرَةِ حَسَنَةً وَقِنَا عَذَابَ النَّارِ "Our Lord, give us in this world that which is good and in the Hereafter that which is good, and save us from the punishment of the Fire." (Surah Al-Baqarah 2:201)

Practical Daily Lessons

  • Firm Faith in Times of Uncertainty: Recognizing that every delay or hardship in life carries divine wisdom beyond immediate human comprehension.
  • The Power of Silent Supplication: Remembering Allah through sincere invocation (Dua) calms anxious thoughts and restores inner peace.
  • Consistency in Righteous Action: Small, consistent good deeds—reciting sacred verses, making Istighfar, and extending kindness—bring immense spiritual barakah.

Frequently Asked Questions

What is the central theme of Surah Al-Baqara (Ayah 282)?

The primary theme focuses on spiritual wisdom & guidance, guiding believers to cultivate unwavering trust in Allah, practice patience through trials, and find solace in divine remembrance.

How can I apply Surah Al-Baqara in my daily life for mental peace?

Recite these verses deliberately after daily prayers or during night reflection (Tahajjud). Contemplate both the Urdu and English meanings to internalize the divine promise of relief after hardship.

Where was Surah Al-Baqara revealed and how many verses does it contain?

Surah Al-Baqara is chapter #2 of the Holy Quran, containing sacred revelations that address both personal spiritual development and communal ethics.

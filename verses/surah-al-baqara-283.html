Surah Al-Baqara (Ayah 283) — Arabic, Translation & Spiritual Tafsir

Published 2026-11-14 Surah #2 4 min read
۞ وَإِن كُنتُمْ عَلَىٰ سَفَرٍۢ وَلَمْ تَجِدُوا۟ كَاتِبًۭا فَرِهَٰنٌۭ مَّقْبُوضَةٌۭ ۖ فَإِنْ أَمِنَ بَعْضُكُم بَعْضًۭا فَلْيُؤَدِّ ٱلَّذِى ٱؤْتُمِنَ أَمَٰنَتَهُۥ وَلْيَتَّقِ ٱللَّهَ رَبَّهُۥ ۗ وَلَا تَكْتُمُوا۟ ٱلشَّهَٰدَةَ ۚ وَمَن يَكْتُمْهَا فَإِنَّهُۥٓ ءَاثِمٌۭ قَلْبُهُۥ ۗ وَٱللَّهُ بِمَا تَعْمَلُونَ عَلِيمٌۭ ﴿283﴾
Listen to Recitation (Mishary Rashid Alafasy)
اردو ترجمہ (Kanzul Iman / Jalandhry)
اور اگر تم سفر میں ہو اور لکھنے والا نہ پاؤ تو گِرو (رہن) ہو قبضہ میں دیا ہوا اور اگر تم ایک کو دوسرے پر اطمینان ہو تو وہ جسے اس نے امین سمجھا تھا اپنی امانت ادا کردے اور اللہ سے ڈرے جو اس کا رب ہے اور گواہی نہ چھپاؤ اور جو گواہی چھپائے گا تو اندر سے اسکا دل گنہگار ہے اور اللہ تمہارے کاموں کو جانتا ہے،
English Translation (Sahih International)
"And if you are on a journey and cannot find a scribe, then a security deposit [should be] taken. And if one of you entrusts another, then let him who is entrusted discharge his trust [faithfully] and let him fear Allah, his Lord. And do not conceal testimony, for whoever conceals it - his heart is indeed sinful, and Allah is Knowing of what you do."

Spiritual Context & Meaning

Surah Al-Baqara holds profound significance in Islamic spirituality, offering timeless reassurance to believers. In these sacred verses (Ayah 283), the Divine address directly touches the human heart, speaking directly to our inner struggles, trials, and search for spiritual grounding. When life presents overwhelming hardships, turning to the revelation provides clarity, reminding the believer of Allah's infinite wisdom, boundless mercy, and watchful care over all affairs.

Practical Dua for Peace & Barakah:
رَبَّنَا آتِنَا فِي الدُّنْيَا حَسَنَةً وَفِي الآخِرَةِ حَسَنَةً وَقِنَا عَذَابَ النَّارِ "Our Lord, give us in this world that which is good and in the Hereafter that which is good, and save us from the punishment of the Fire." (Surah Al-Baqarah 2:201)

Practical Daily Lessons

  • Firm Faith in Times of Uncertainty: Recognizing that every delay or hardship in life carries divine wisdom beyond immediate human comprehension.
  • The Power of Silent Supplication: Remembering Allah through sincere invocation (Dua) calms anxious thoughts and restores inner peace.
  • Consistency in Righteous Action: Small, consistent good deeds—reciting sacred verses, making Istighfar, and extending kindness—bring immense spiritual barakah.

Frequently Asked Questions

What is the central theme of Surah Al-Baqara (Ayah 283)?

The primary theme focuses on spiritual wisdom & guidance, guiding believers to cultivate unwavering trust in Allah, practice patience through trials, and find solace in divine remembrance.

How can I apply Surah Al-Baqara in my daily life for mental peace?

Recite these verses deliberately after daily prayers or during night reflection (Tahajjud). Contemplate both the Urdu and English meanings to internalize the divine promise of relief after hardship.

Where was Surah Al-Baqara revealed and how many verses does it contain?

Surah Al-Baqara is chapter #2 of the Holy Quran, containing sacred revelations that address both personal spiritual development and communal ethics.
